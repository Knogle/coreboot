## Proven path

The transcript establishes one continuous execution of all of the following:

1. three CSI phases, with pass 3 returning a self-consistent result;
2. one guarded MINIT call returning `EAX=0`;
3. the existing hard return gate and provisional automatic handoff;
4. complete uncached destructive tests of `0x00000000..0x0009ffff`,
   `0x01000000..0x017fffff` and `0x02000000..0x027fffff`, including alias
   checks and clearing;
5. version-9 CBMEM handoff, postcar and DRAM-backed coreboot ramstage;
6. `IOHBUSNO` changing from `0000` to `0100` and removal of sampled aliases;
7. the Radeon `1002:68f9`, coreboot resource assignment and execution of its
   physical 59,904-byte VBIOS;
8. B06VP LAPIC state changing from masked LINTs to unmasked LINT0 ExtINT and
   LINT1 NMI with the exact post-state gate passing;
9. SeaBIOS 1.17.0 progressing through its timer-dependent boot sequence;
10. execution of the RTL8168 iPXE ROM on `02:00.0`;
11. Ethernet link-up, DHCP address `192.0.2.196`, gateway `192.0.2.1`;
12. TFTP download of `netboot.xyz.kpxe`, 388,848 bytes, from
    `192.0.2.53`;
13. execution of netboot.xyz v3.0.0 and display of its menu.

This closes the B06VP timer-delivery hypothesis: the previous B06VO wait was
caused by the missing BSP virtual-wire LAPIC setup, or at minimum B06VP's exact
LAPIC delta was sufficient to remove that boundary.

## USB and keyboard observation

SeaBIOS initialized:

```text
EHCI 00:1a.7 and 00:1d.7
UHCI 00:1a.0, 00:1a.1, 00:1a.2
UHCI 00:1d.0, 00:1d.1, 00:1d.2
```

No USB descriptor or `USB keyboard initialized` line followed.  The i8042
probe repeatedly read status `ff` and timed out.  The operator subsequently
reported that PS/2 input did not work.  This proves neither that a USB device
was physically connected nor that port power/routing was correct; it does
prove that merely enabling SeaBIOS's UHCI/EHCI/HID configuration was
insufficient in this run.

## Bounded claim

HW-03 is one successful payload/network run, not the required ten cold plus ten
warm boots.  It does not yet prove USB or PS/2 input, SATA/storage operation,
an OS kernel boot, SMP/AP startup, complete ACPI/SMBIOS, SMM/S3, or all DIMM
topologies.  RAM and QPI are accepted only as the current development
assumption while post-SeaBIOS platform work proceeds.
